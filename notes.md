## Magisk (a19666d8) (30700)
- magiskboot: Lower the compression presets for XZ & LZMA<br>magiskboot: Lower the compression presets for XZ & LZMA<br>The compression routine always uses the maximum compression preset for all formats.<br>This is problematic with XZ and LZMA because they use far more memory than the other formats, while just providing a very marginal advantage in the resulting file size.<br>The compression preset was changed from 9 to 6, which is often the default value and provides more than enough compression.<br>Tested on Samsung Galaxy Fame (512MB RAM, perfect for this).
